How to read these numbers
  • The spot boards, the DDR4 line, kit prices and graphics card prices are re-read from their source pages every hour. The DDR5 and NAND trend lines gain a reading each time TrendForce updates its board. The forecasts are saved readings from TrendForce press releases.
  • TrendForce prints its spot boards without a currency. Outlets quoting the same boards report US dollars per chip, and this page follows them.
  • The DDR4 line is Capital & Compute's log of TrendForce and DRAMeXchange readings. A stretch of more than 45 days with no free reading is drawn dotted.
  • The saved DDR5 16Gb readings come from TrendForce and two publishers quoting its board on other days.
  • Kit prices are listings aggregated by WhereIsMyRAM, which resamples its own history on each refresh.
  • Amazon prices include marketplace sellers and one import listing. Arc B570 and Arc B580 had no new stock.
  • The Newegg index is a pilot. RTX 5090 had no qualifying Newegg offer and is outside it.
  • Left out: memoryindex.io price levels, which disagree with the boards they cite, and street price roundups with no dates.
