    How to read these numbers
    • A verdict says where a price is in its cycle. A price within 2% of its high is at it, and one 10% or more below is past its peak. Stalled means it rose 3% or more in three months and is steady this month. Falling faster means more than half of the three-month fall came in the last month. The high is the highest reading this page holds, which starts in January 2025 for spot prices, April 2025 for the kit and January 2026 for graphics cards.
    • By the gigabit, a DDR4 8Gb chip price is divided by 8 and a DDR5 16Gb chip price by 16. The build cost pairs the cheapest 32GB kit with the best US price of one card, and compares it with the first day that card has a price here and the kit reading nearest to that day.
    • A change of 1% or more reads Rising or Falling, and less reads Steady. Green and red say what a move means for someone building a PC. They are not a forecast.
    • Each period compares the latest reading with the one nearest to that many days earlier. It reads No reading when none lies within 3 days for a week, 10 for a month, 15 for three months or 31 for a year. Hold the pointer over a figure for the earlier price and its date.
    • Spot is the open-market price for immediate delivery. Retail is what a builder pays.
    • Spot prices before this page began reading the boards come from copies of the same DRAMeXchange board kept by the Internet Archive. A copy holds whichever session was on the board when it was taken, and days without a copy are gaps. A gap of more than 45 days is drawn dotted.
    • TrendForce prints its spot boards without a currency. Outlets quoting the same boards report US dollars, and this page follows them.
    • The graphics card figure is the median over current-generation cards in stock on Tom's Hardware's tracker. A period figure is the median of each card's own price change. The tracker began in January 2026, so there is no year figure yet.
    • Kit prices are listings aggregated by WhereIsMyRAM, which resamples its own history on each refresh.
    • The Newegg index is a pilot. RTX 5090 had no qualifying Newegg offer and is outside it.
    • The forecasts are saved readings from TrendForce press releases.
